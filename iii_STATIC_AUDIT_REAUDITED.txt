FIA power and energy distinction
--------------------------------
The 2012 rules specified a maximum KERS power of 60 kW and a separate
400 kJ-per-lap released-energy limit, measured at the connection to the rear
wheel drivetrain.

The extracted KERS durations total 6.9805 s. Full saturation at 60 kW over
all extracted windows would permit 418.83 kJ. The OCP does not force full
saturation and therefore does not necessarily use this energy, but it also
does not explicitly enforce the 400 kJ integral limit. This omission is
intentional to preserve the user's requested minimal scope.
